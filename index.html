Programme overview

EXCELS at a glance

EXCELS is a University of Siegen initiative that prepares high-performing international Bachelor’s students for research-oriented Master’s study. It connects subject-specific preparation with academic orientation and direct insight into Siegen’s research environment.

Who it is for
International Bachelor’s students approaching the end of a relevant degree.
Master’s direction
Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Mechatronics at the University of Siegen.
Research focus
Artificial Intelligence and intelligent sensor systems.
Programme format
Digital Prep, plus a separately selected 14-day Summer School cohort in Siegen.

Early fit check

Is EXCELS for you?

EXCELS is being designed for students whose academic background, study stage, and research interests align with the project. Use these points as an initial guide, not as final application criteria.

  • Study stageYou are in the penultimate or final year of a Bachelor’s degree.
  • Academic backgroundYou study Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a closely related STEM subject.
  • Academic strengthYou have a strong academic record and a solid foundation in your subject.
  • Research directionYou are seriously interested in AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics and in research-oriented Master’s study.
  • Programme fitYou are ready for active online preparation and, if separately selected, a 14-day Summer School in Siegen.

The EXCELS pathway

Prepare online. Progress through separate selection. Experience research in Siegen.

EXCELS is planned as two linked but separately selected formats. Participation in Digital Prep does not automatically lead to a Summer School invitation or admission to a Master’s programme.

  1. Stage 01

    Digital Prep Online

    Build a shared foundation

    Entry to Digital Prep is planned to be based primarily on prior academic performance. The online preparation then combines subject knowledge, academic orientation, practical guidance, and early contact with the University of Siegen.

    • Modules in AI and sensor systems
    • German teaching, learning, and examination culture
    • Application and administrative guidance
    • Exchange with teachers and researchers

    Planned annual cohort30–50 participants

  2. Stage 02

    Summer School selection Separate decision

    Selection based on quality and fit

    Most Summer School invitees are expected to come from Digital Prep, but progression is not automatic. The planned review is expected to consider factors including:

    • Academic performance and mathematical or algorithmic foundations
    • Research interest and thematic fit
    • Motivation, development potential, and active preparation
    • Application documents and a short online technical interview

    Final criteriaTo be confirmed with the application information

  3. Stage 03

    14-day Summer School In Siegen

    Experience research as a cohort

    From 2027, a selected cohort is planned to spend 14 days at the University of Siegen, with Summer Schools expected in June or July through 2029.

    • Project-based teamwork
    • Laboratory visits and research demonstrations
    • ZESS Research Forum and researcher exchange
    • Peer networking and a cultural programme

    Planned annual cohort15 participants

Linked to ZESS

A direct view into research culture

The Summer School is planned to connect with the annual ZESS Research Forum, where doctoral researchers share their work through talks and poster presentations. Structured conversations are intended to help participants meet researchers, explore topics, and understand potential research pathways.

Cost information

No participation fee is currently planned for Digital Prep or the Summer School. The exact scope of travel and accommodation support and all participation conditions will be confirmed with the application information.

Support and student life

Prepare for the transition. Build connections that last.

EXCELS is designed to support selected participants beyond subject preparation—through planned academic mentoring, international guidance, and deliberate connections to researchers and peers as they move towards possible Master’s study in Siegen.

  1. 01

    Post-selection transition

    Academic mentoring

    Selected students in the planned transition phase will receive a personal mentor from ETI or ZESS before the start of studies. Individual development planning and workshops in academic work, data analysis, and publication processes are intended to help them identify realistic next steps.

  2. 02

    During Digital Prep

    International transition

    ETI and the International Office are planned to introduce German teaching, learning, and examination culture and provide guidance on applications, admission, study organisation, and administrative procedures. The project plan also includes German-language learning and intercultural formats to support integration.

  3. 03

    Across the pathway

    Research and peer networks

    Moderated exchange, the Summer School, Research Ask-Me-Anything sessions, Junior Meets Senior, and Alumni Stories are planned to connect participants with teachers, researchers, peers, and role models as they explore possible study and research paths in Siegen.

Who receives what?

Digital Prep guidance is planned for programme participants. Personal mentoring belongs to the later transition phase for selected students. Exact availability and conditions will be published before applications open.

Decision path

Three decisions. No automatic progression.

This is the planned route. Final rules and the precise application sequence will be confirmed in the official call.

  1. Decision 01 EXCELS

    Digital Prep selection

    Selection is planned to focus primarily on previous academic performance. The final threshold, evidence, eligible group, and annual dates will be stated in the call.

    Outcome: access to the online preparation programme
  2. Decision 02 EXCELS

    Summer School invitation

    A separate review is planned to consider academic excellence, technical foundations, research fit, motivation, and development potential. Qualified candidates are expected to complete a brief online technical interview.

    Outcome: invitation to the selected in-person cohort
  3. Decision 03 University

    Master’s admission

    Admission is a separate University process with programme-specific requirements and dates. An EXCELS place does not replace the University application or guarantee admission.

    Outcome: an independent degree-programme decision

Independent University process

EXCELS selection is not Master’s admission.

International Master’s applications are submitted through the University’s UNISONO portal during the relevant programme’s application period. Requirements and dates differ by programme and applicant category. Admission to a degree programme likewise does not automatically include an EXCELS or Summer School place.

Application questions

Answers before you apply.

These answers reflect the current project plan. The published EXCELS call will be the definitive source for eligibility, dates, documents, and selection rules.

Can I apply to EXCELS now?

No. EXCELS applications are not currently open, and no application link or deadline has been published. UNISONO is the University’s degree-application portal; it is not an EXCELS application form.

Can I apply directly to the Summer School?

The final application route has not yet been confirmed. The project plan expects most Summer School invitees to come from Digital Prep. If a direct route is offered, it will be stated explicitly in the official call.

Does Digital Prep guarantee a Summer School place?

No. Summer School selection is a separate decision based on quality and fit. Active participation in Digital Prep may form part of that review, but progression is not automatic.

What is planned to be assessed for the Summer School?

The planned review considers academic performance, mathematical and algorithmic foundations, research interest and fit in AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics, motivation, development potential, and active preparation. Final criteria and weighting will be published with the application information.

Which documents will I need?

The final checklist is not yet available. The project plan anticipates application documents and a letter of motivation for the Summer School review, but exact files, formats, language requirements, translations, and certification rules remain to be confirmed.

Will there be an interview?

A brief online technical interview is planned for qualified Summer School candidates to discuss motivation and fit. The format, timing, platform, and invitation process will be confirmed in the official call.

Does EXCELS guarantee admission to a Master’s programme?

No. EXCELS selection and admission to a Master’s programme are separate processes. An EXCELS place does not replace a University application or guarantee admission. Admission to a degree programme likewise does not automatically include an EXCELS or Summer School place.

When are the Summer Schools planned?

A 14-day Summer School is planned for June or July in 2027, 2028, and 2029, with a cohort of 15 participants expected each year. Exact dates and all application deadlines remain to be announced.

Is there a participation fee, and what travel support is included?

No participation fee is currently planned for Digital Prep or the Summer School. Travel and accommodation support is planned for the selected Summer School cohort, but exact coverage, booking arrangements, and participation conditions will be confirmed with the application information. This does not describe the separate costs of Master’s enrolment or living in Siegen.

Can students outside the named priority countries apply?

The final country scope has not yet been published. The countries named earlier on this page are outreach priorities in the project plan; they should not be treated as confirmed eligibility rules.
