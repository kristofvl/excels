Early fit check

Who EXCELS is for.

EXCELS suits students whose study stage, subject, and research interests match the six points below.

  • Study stageYou are in the penultimate or final year of a Bachelor’s degree.
  • Academic backgroundYou study Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a closely related STEM subject.
  • Academic strengthYour grades are strong and your subject foundations are solid.
  • EnglishYou can study confidently in English. The required level and accepted evidence are set in the official call.
  • Research directionYou are seriously interested in AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics and in research-oriented Master’s study.
  • CommitmentYou are ready for active online preparation and, if separately selected, a 14-day Summer School in Siegen.

The EXCELS pathway

Online first, then Siegen.

EXCELS starts with online Digital Prep. A separate selection then decides the 15 Summer School places in Siegen. Master’s admission and the ZESS stipend are separate decisions again.

  1. Stage 01

    Apply

    Apply for Digital Prep during the application period. Selection is based mainly on your academic performance.

  2. Stage 02

    Digital Prep

    Complete online modules in AI and sensor systems, learn how studying in Germany works, and exchange ideas with researchers.

  3. Stage 03

    Summer School

    A separate selection considers your documents, academic strength, research fit, and a short technical interview. The 15 selected participants spend 14 days in Siegen on project work, lab visits, and research exchange.

  4. Stage 04

    Master’s study

    You apply for your Master’s programme separately, and the University decides admission. After enrolment, up to two participants may be selected for a ZESS stipend of €992 per month for up to 24 months.

Independent University process

EXCELS selection is not Master’s admission.

Taking part in Digital Prep or the Summer School does not replace a University application or guarantee admission. Likewise, admission to a degree programme does not automatically include an EXCELS or Summer School place.

Application guide

Your EXCELS application, step by step.

Digital Prep and the Summer School have separate selections. The official call for the 2026/27 round will confirm eligibility, required documents, and deadlines; until then, use this guide to prepare.

How to prepare

Five steps to a complete application

  1. Step 01

    Check your fit

    Use the fit check to review your study stage, academic background, academic strength, and research direction.

  2. Step 02

    Choose a Master’s direction

    Compare the Master’s programmes and their individual admission requirements.

  3. Step 03

    Read the call

    When the official call is published, check eligibility, required documents, and dates.

  4. Step 04

    Gather your documents

    Prepare the documents listed below so they are ready to upload.

  5. Step 05

    Apply

    Submit your application and documents through the official EXCELS form before the deadline.

Documents

Documents to prepare

  • Academic CV

    Prepare a concise overview of your education, relevant coursework, technical skills, projects, research experience, internships, and other relevant experience.

  • Academic record and degree status

    Have a current transcript or equivalent academic record ready, together with evidence of enrolment or completion. It should make your grades and your mathematical or algorithmic preparation clear.

  • Technical and research work

    Collect relevant project summaries, thesis work, publications, presentations, research placements, internships, or comparable technical work. State your own contribution and how it connects to AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics.

  • Motivation statement

    Explain why EXCELS and research-oriented Master’s study in Siegen fit your background, interests, development goals, and the technology career you intend to pursue.

  • Additional evidence

    Prepare any language evidence, translations, recommendations, certificates, or background information the official call asks for, and follow its requirements exactly.

Application form

Prepare the documents above, then submit them through the official EXCELS application form during the application period.

Applicant guidance

Questions and answers

Answers grouped by topic. If an answer differs from the official call for a round, the call applies.

01 · Before you apply: Eligibility and study routes
Is EXCELS a degree programme, or do I apply separately for a Master’s?

EXCELS is not a degree programme. It prepares you for Master’s study through online Digital Prep, a separately selected Summer School, and support during the transition. You apply for a Master’s programme separately and must meet its admission and enrolment requirements.

Who should consider EXCELS?

High-performing international Bachelor’s students nearing the end of a relevant degree who want to explore research-oriented Master’s study in Siegen. A strong interest in artificial intelligence, sensor systems, or microelectronics is essential. The official call for each round defines who can apply.

Can I take part before completing my Bachelor’s degree?

Yes. EXCELS is aimed at Bachelor’s students in the last two years of their degree. You show your current academic standing as the call requires; admission to a Master’s programme later depends on meeting that programme’s requirements.

Which academic backgrounds are relevant?

Computer science, electrical engineering, mechatronics, and closely related STEM fields are the clearest starting points. The fit check explains the expected academic strength and research direction.

How do priority countries and my current place of study affect eligibility?

EXCELS focuses its outreach on selected universities and regions in Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa, but that focus is not itself an eligibility rule. The Academic Horizons programme aims to bring prospective international Master’s students to Germany, so it is not intended for students already studying at a German university. The official call states the exact country, institution, and residence conditions.

Which Master’s programmes can I aim for?

EXCELS connects with three English-taught Master’s programmes: Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, and Mechatronic Engineering. Each has its own subject and English-language requirements, and each programme’s examination board decides admission independently.

02 · Build your case: Preparing an application
When and where do I submit an EXCELS application?

Through a dedicated University of Siegen MoveON form during the application period, planned for October–November 2026. The form link and exact deadline will be published on this page. Do not use any other MoveON form; your Master’s application is a separate process.

Do I apply for a particular research project?

No. EXCELS is built around a shared AI and sensor-systems pathway rather than a list of individual research projects. Describe a clear research direction, and follow any topic-choice instructions in the official call.

What evidence should I begin preparing?

Start with a concise academic CV, a current transcript and evidence of your degree status, examples of relevant technical or research work, and a focused motivation statement. The document list helps you prepare; the official call confirms which documents are required.

What should my CV and motivation statement make clear?

Your CV should show your preparation through specific coursework, technical skills, projects, internships, thesis work, or research experience, including your own contribution. Your motivation statement should connect that evidence to the EXCELS research focus, your preferred study direction in Siegen, and your goals.

Can I demonstrate potential without formal research experience?

Yes. A research job or a publication is not the only evidence of potential. A substantial thesis, capstone, laboratory assignment, course project, internship, or self-directed technical investigation can be valuable when you explain the problem, your decisions, your individual contribution, and what you learned.

Which language evidence, translations, or recommendations do I need?

You need strong English. The official call sets the required level and the accepted evidence for EXCELS, such as a test certificate or confirmation that your Bachelor’s programme is taught in English, along with any translations or recommendations. Master’s programmes have their own language requirements, and an English-taught degree does not automatically replace a language test; check the ETI application overview.

03 · Two decisions: Selection and progression
How are Digital Prep participants selected?

Mainly on your academic performance, especially your grades. Successful applicants are usually in the top quarter of their year or can show that they are among the stronger students in their programme. The official call sets out the exact criteria.

How are Summer School places decided, and is there an interview?

A separate review considers academic excellence, mathematical and algorithmic foundations, research fit, relevant work, motivation, development potential, and your active participation in Digital Prep. Shortlisted candidates have a short online technical interview, and representatives of ETI, ZESS, and the International Office then decide together.

Does completing Digital Prep secure a Summer School place?

No. Digital Prep and the Summer School have separate selection decisions. Taking part can strengthen the evidence of your active preparation, but it does not guarantee a place in the in-person cohort.

Can someone be considered for the Summer School without Digital Prep?

Most Summer School participants are expected to come from Digital Prep. Do not assume there is a direct route; the official call will say whether any other route is open.

Can circumstances affecting my academic record be considered?

Yes. The Summer School review is transparent and non-discriminatory. Alongside academic strength, it can recognise educational barriers you have overcome, intercultural competence, and social engagement. Share any context or sensitive information only in the way the official call describes.

What if I am not selected for the Summer School or admitted to a Master’s programme?

Each decision applies only to its own process. Not receiving a Summer School invitation is not a Master’s rejection, so you can still apply for a degree programme if you are eligible. Equally, EXCELS participation cannot override an examination board’s admission decision or guarantee a place on a degree.

04 · Know what to expect: Participation and support
What happens during Digital Prep?

Online modules in AI and sensor systems, an introduction to teaching, learning, and exams at German universities, practical guidance, and moderated exchange with lecturers and researchers. The official call gives the schedule, platform, and expected workload.

Can the Summer School be completed remotely?

No. Digital Prep is the online stage; the Summer School takes place in Siegen over 14 days for a cohort of 15. See the timeline for 2027. Later rounds are expected in June or July 2028 and 2029.

Which participation costs does EXCELS cover?

Digital Prep and the Summer School have no participation fee, and the selected Summer School cohort receives support for travel and accommodation. The official call explains which costs are covered and how. EXCELS never charges application fees, so be cautious of anyone who asks you to pay for a place.

EXCELS does not fund a whole Master’s degree. The University currently charges no tuition, but enrolled students pay a semester contribution and need their own funds for everyday living, health insurance, and any immigration requirements; use the University’s financing guidance when planning.

Does EXCELS guarantee a scholarship, admission, internship, or job?

No. EXCELS is not a general Master’s scholarship and does not guarantee degree admission, an internship, or employment. The ZESS stipend is a separate, limited award for up to two selected newly enrolled international Master’s students, so do not plan your study finances on EXCELS participation alone. Personal mentoring is offered in a later, selected transition phase rather than to every Digital Prep participant.

Who is responsible for visas, insurance, and accommodation?

You are responsible for meeting immigration and insurance requirements, and selection for EXCELS does not guarantee a visa. A short Summer School visit and later degree study may require different immigration steps; consult the responsible German diplomatic mission and the University’s visa and residence guidance.

Degree students arrange their own housing, and admission does not include a room. Use the University’s accommodation guidance; Summer School travel and accommodation arrangements are shared separately with the selected cohort.

Where should I take a question that is not answered here?

Use Ask us about EXCELS at the end of this page. For Master’s applications, see the ETI application guidance; for arrival and international-student services, contact the International Office.

Project and funding

EXCELS at the University of Siegen.

EXCELS is led by the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (ETI), working with the Center for Sensor Systems (ZESS) and the International Office.

EXCELS is funded by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) with funds from the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR) through the programme Academic Horizons – Attracting Global Minds, part of the 1.000-Köpfe-Plus-Programm.
