International study and research pathway

Your pathway to research-oriented Master’s study in AI and intelligent sensor systems

EXCELS prepares selected international Bachelor’s students for Master’s programmes in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, and Mechatronics through Digital Prep, a research-oriented Summer School in Siegen, and early connections to researchers at ETI and ZESS.

Programme overview

EXCELS at a glance

EXCELS is a University of Siegen initiative that prepares high-performing international Bachelor’s students for research-oriented Master’s study. It connects subject-specific preparation with academic orientation and direct insight into Siegen’s research environment.

Who it is for
International Bachelor’s students approaching the end of a relevant degree.
Master’s direction
Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Mechatronics at the University of Siegen.
Research focus
Artificial Intelligence and intelligent sensor systems.
Programme format
Digital Prep, plus a separately selected 14-day Summer School cohort in Siegen.

Early fit check

Is EXCELS for you?

EXCELS is being designed for students whose academic background, study stage, and research interests align with the project. Use these points as an initial guide, not as final application criteria.

  • Study stageYou are in the penultimate or final year of a Bachelor’s degree.
  • Academic backgroundYou study Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a closely related STEM subject.
  • Academic strengthYou have a strong academic record and a solid foundation in your subject.
  • Research directionYou are seriously interested in AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics and in research-oriented Master’s study.
  • Programme fitYou are ready for active online preparation and, if separately selected, a 14-day Summer School in Siegen.

The EXCELS pathway

Prepare online. Progress through separate selection. Experience research in Siegen.

EXCELS is planned as two linked but separately selected formats. Participation in Digital Prep does not automatically lead to a Summer School invitation or admission to a Master’s programme.

  1. Stage 01

    Digital Prep Online

    Build a shared foundation

    Entry to Digital Prep is planned to be based primarily on prior academic performance. The online preparation then combines subject knowledge, academic orientation, practical guidance, and early contact with the University of Siegen.

    • Modules in AI and sensor systems
    • German teaching, learning, and examination culture
    • Application and administrative guidance
    • Exchange with teachers and researchers

    Planned annual cohort30–50 participants

  2. Stage 02

    Summer School selection Separate decision

    Selection based on quality and fit

    Most Summer School invitees are expected to come from Digital Prep, but progression is not automatic. The planned review is expected to consider factors including:

    • Academic performance and mathematical or algorithmic foundations
    • Research interest and thematic fit
    • Motivation, development potential, and active preparation
    • Application documents and a short online technical interview

    Planned selection routeEvidence review; interview for qualified candidates

  3. Stage 03

    14-day Summer School In Siegen

    Experience research as a cohort

    From 2027, a selected cohort is planned to spend 14 days at the University of Siegen, with Summer Schools expected in June or July through 2029.

    • Project-based teamwork
    • Laboratory visits and research demonstrations
    • ZESS Research Forum and researcher exchange
    • Peer networking and a cultural programme

    Planned annual cohort15 participants

Linked to ZESS

A direct view into research culture

The Summer School is planned to connect with the annual ZESS Research Forum, where doctoral researchers share their work through talks and poster presentations. Structured conversations are intended to help participants meet researchers, explore topics, and understand potential research pathways.

Cost information

No participation fee is planned for Digital Prep or the Summer School. The applicable EXCELS information defines the exact scope of travel and accommodation support and all participation conditions for each intake.

Separate admissions

Support and student life

Prepare for the transition. Build connections that last.

EXCELS is designed to support selected participants beyond subject preparation—through planned academic mentoring, international guidance, and deliberate connections to researchers and peers as they move towards possible Master’s study in Siegen.

  1. 01

    Post-selection transition

    Academic mentoring

    Selected students in the planned transition phase will receive a personal mentor from ETI or ZESS before the start of studies. Individual development planning and workshops in academic work, data analysis, and publication processes are intended to help them identify realistic next steps.

  2. 02

    During Digital Prep

    International transition

    ETI and the International Office are planned to introduce German teaching, learning, and examination culture and provide guidance on applications, admission, study organisation, and administrative procedures. The project plan also includes German-language learning and intercultural formats to support integration.

  3. 03

    Across the pathway

    Research and peer networks

    Moderated exchange, the Summer School, Research Ask-Me-Anything sessions, Junior Meets Senior, and Alumni Stories are planned to connect participants with teachers, researchers, peers, and role models as they explore possible study and research paths in Siegen.

Who receives what?

Digital Prep guidance is planned for programme participants. Personal mentoring belongs to the later transition phase for selected students. The applicable programme information defines exact availability and conditions.

Application guide

Your EXCELS application, step by step.

EXCELS uses a two-stage selection process: first for Digital Prep, then separately for the research-oriented Summer School. The applicable EXCELS application and selection information defines the binding eligibility rules, required evidence, dates, and participation route for each intake.

Application checklist

Prepare in one clear order.

  1. Step 01

    Check your fit

    Use the early fit guide to review your study stage, academic background, academic strength, and research direction.

  2. Step 02

    Review your Master’s direction

    Compare Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, and Mechatronics, including each programme’s independent admission requirements.

  3. Step 03

    Read the applicable EXCELS information

    Confirm the applicant scope, eligibility rules, evidence, dates, and application or nomination route that apply to that intake.

  4. Step 04

    Prepare and verify your evidence

    Organise material that demonstrates academic performance, technical foundations, relevant interests or projects, motivation, and development goals. Follow only the evidence requirements stated for that intake.

  5. Step 05

    Follow the stated route

    Use the application or nomination route specified for the relevant intake. The University’s Master’s application is a separate process.

Application materials

Have these core items ready.

This preparation list reflects the planned EXCELS selection criteria. The applicable intake information remains definitive for the exact files, formats, and language or translation rules.

  1. Item 01

    Academic CV

    Prepare a concise overview of your education, relevant coursework, technical skills, projects, research exposure, internships, and other relevant experience.

  2. Item 02

    Academic record and degree status

    Have a current transcript or equivalent academic record ready, together with evidence of enrolment or completion. It should make your grades and relevant mathematical or algorithmic preparation clear.

  3. Item 03

    Technical and research work

    Collect relevant project summaries, thesis work, publications, presentations, research placements, internships, or comparable technical work. State your own contribution and its connection to AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics.

  4. Item 04

    Motivation letter

    Explain why EXCELS and research-oriented Master’s study in Siegen fit your background, interests, development goals, and intended technology-oriented career.

  5. Item 05

    Intake-specific supporting evidence

    Prepare any language evidence, translations, recommendations, certificates, or contextual information requested for the intake, following the stated requirements exactly.

Selection

How applications are assessed.

The project plan defines two distinct EXCELS decisions.

Stage 01

Digital Prep selection

Previous academic performance, particularly grades, is the planned primary basis. The project plan describes candidates as generally being in the top quartile of their cohort or demonstrably among the stronger students in their programme.

Planned annual group30–50 Digital Prep participants

Stage 02

Summer School selection

The separate review is planned to consider academic excellence, mathematical and algorithmic foundations, research interest and thematic fit, relevant projects, motivation, development potential, and active participation in Digital Prep.

Qualified candidates are expected to complete a brief online technical interview. The final decision is planned jointly by representatives of ETI, ZESS, and the International Office.

Planned annual group15 Summer School participants

Separate selection

Most Summer School invitees are expected to come from Digital Prep, but Digital Prep participation does not guarantee an invitation.

Independent University process

EXCELS selection is not Master’s admission.

Participation in Digital Prep or the Summer School does not replace a University application or guarantee admission. Admission to a degree programme likewise does not automatically include an EXCELS or Summer School place. Follow the University’s current programme-specific requirements.

Application questions

Frequently asked questions.

The applicable EXCELS application and selection information is definitive for each intake. These answers explain the stable programme structure.

Who is EXCELS designed for?

EXCELS is designed for high-performing international Bachelor’s students in the penultimate or final year of a relevant degree, with strong interest in AI, sensor systems, or microelectronics and research-oriented Master’s study in Siegen. The applicable selection information defines the binding applicant scope for each intake.

Does Digital Prep guarantee a Summer School place?

No. Summer School selection is a separate decision based on quality and fit. Active participation in Digital Prep may form part of that review, but progression is not automatic.

How is the Summer School cohort selected?

The planned review considers academic performance, mathematical and algorithmic foundations, research interest and thematic fit, relevant projects, motivation, development potential, and active preparation. Qualified candidates are expected to complete a brief online technical interview, with the final decision planned jointly by ETI, ZESS, and the International Office.

Is the Master’s application part of EXCELS?

No. EXCELS selection and admission to a University of Siegen Master’s programme are separate processes. Participation in Digital Prep or the Summer School does not replace a degree application or guarantee admission, and degree admission does not automatically include an EXCELS place.

Is there a participation fee, and what support is planned?

No participation fee is planned for Digital Prep or the Summer School. The project also plans travel and accommodation support for the selected Summer School cohort. The applicable EXCELS information defines the exact coverage and participation conditions for each intake; separate Master’s enrolment and living costs are not included in this statement.
