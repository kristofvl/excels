Taking part

How to join

  • Register by Monday 30 November, with the same name and email address you used in your EXCELS application, so we know it’s you.
  • Zoom then shows your personal link and emails it to you. If the email doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder.
  • Your link works for every session. It’s only for you; sharing it is not allowed.
  • Update your Zoom app to the latest version before 1 December, because older versions may not work properly in the sessions. If you can’t update it, join in your web browser instead.
  • Join a few minutes early to check your microphone, camera and connection.
  • Look at this page again before each day, in case a time has changed.

We’ll post the Zoom registration link here before the first session.

About the speakers

Meet the professors

  • Prof. Jasmin Grosinger

    Professor of Communication Engineering

    Prof. Grosinger’s research focuses on energy-efficient and sustainable wireless communication, from batteryless sensors to highly integrated wireless systems for the Internet of Things.

    Prof. Grosinger’s profile→

  • Prof. Jovita Lukasik

    Assistant Professor for Visual Computing

    Prof. Lukasik heads the Visual Computing group, with a research focus on efficiency and robustness in neural architecture search and computer vision.

    Prof. Lukasik’s website→

  • Prof. Kristof Van Laerhoven

    Professor for Ubiquitous Computing

    Prof. Van Laerhoven’s research focuses on wearable sensing systems, activity recognition, wireless sensor networks, machine learning and signal processing.
